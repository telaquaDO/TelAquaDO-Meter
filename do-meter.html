Why dissolved oxygen

Your stock breathes the water.

Dissolved oxygen is one of the most important readings in any pond. A quick check tells you whether your shrimp and fish have enough of it.

  • Oxygen changes overnight

    Levels often fall through the night and are lowest around dawn. Checking at the right time shows you the real picture.

  • Run aerators with confidence

    Use readings to decide when aeration is needed, instead of guessing.

  • Healthier stock, better harvests

    Stable oxygen supports feeding and growth. Catch problems before they turn into losses.

What does the Tel-Aqua DO Meter measure?

It measures dissolved oxygen (DO): the amount of oxygen in your pond or tank water that shrimp and fish use to breathe.

When should I check dissolved oxygen?

Oxygen usually drops through the night and is often lowest around dawn. Many farmers check early in the morning and again in the evening, and more often in hot or cloudy weather.

Is it suitable for both shrimp and fish ponds?

Yes. The DO Meter is designed for routine water-quality checks in shrimp and fish ponds and tanks.
