HOW TO CARE

After Every Use

A little care after every use keeps your pH meter accurate for years.

1

RINSE

After every use, rinse the tip in clean water to remove any residue or chemicals.

2

SHAKE

Shake off the excess water. Do not wipe the electrode.

3

KEEP IT MOIST

Keep the tip moist. Never store it dry or let it dry out.

4

NEVER STORE IN PLAIN WATER

Plain water damages the electrode and reduces accuracy.

CALIBRATION&CARE TIPS

CALIBRATION TIPS
  • Recalibrate whenever accuracy is in doubt or the meter does not hold calibration.
  • Use fresh buffer solution.
  • Rinse the electrode between steps.
  • Calibrate before measuring.
  • Same process for shrimp & fish ponds.
CARE TIPS
  • Rinse after every use.
  • Never let the electrode dry.
  • Never store in plain water.
  • Keep the tip moist with pH 4.00 buffer or proper storage solution.
