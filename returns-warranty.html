Warranty

We build instruments for farmers, and we stand behind them.

7 days — no questions

If anything is wrong with your meter within 7 days of delivery, tell us and we will replace it.

You do not need to explain.

12 months — your meter

If it will not switch on, the display fails, the buttons stop responding, or it will not hold calibration, we replace it free for 12 months from the day you register it.

6 months — your electrode

The electrode is the sensitive glass tip that measures your water.

It is covered for 6 months against manufacturing defects.

Please read this — the most important thing about your meter

The electrode must never be allowed to dry out.

After every use:

  • Rinse the tip in clean water.
  • Shake off the excess.
  • Put the cap back on with a little storage solution or buffer inside.

Never store it dry.

Never store it in plain water — plain water damages the electrode.

Stored correctly, your electrode will work for a year or more.

Stored dry, it can be ruined in a few weeks.

This is true of every pH meter in the world, from the cheapest to the most expensive laboratory instrument.

It is how the technology works, not a fault in the product.

An electrode damaged by dry storage is not covered by warranty — but you do not need a new meter.

Replacement electrodes are available from us at a fair price.

Not Covered

The following are not covered:

  • Drops and physical damage
  • Water entering beyond the rated depth
  • Battery leakage or wrong batteries
  • Opening or tampering with the meter
  • Electrode damage from dry storage or storage in plain water
  • Normal electrode ageing after the covered period
  • Loss or theft
